News, SEC filings, central-bank releases and social posts in; explainable risk signals and stress-tested capital impact out. Every number traceable to its source.

The problem
  • Noise: thousands of items a day; the few that matter (a deposit run, a default, a sanction) look like everything else.
  • No link to the book: news stops at the headline; nobody connects the event to the loans, bonds and equity it moves.
  • Not defensible: a score with no evidence, assumptions or measured accuracy cannot go to a credit committee.

Tripwire answers, for every event: what happened, how much it matters, what it does to this bank's capital, and why we believe it.

  • Company resolution 70.3% · alert precision (standard tier) 74.4%
  • Entity-level sentiment: macro-F1 0.89 vs 0.66 for plain FinBERT (SEntFiN test)
  • Impact: AUC 0.72, Brier 0.139 on 1,003 out-of-time filings
How we keep it honest
  • Each fresh test: articles after the model freeze, labelled by a person, evaluated once
  • Ship a model only if it beats the current one in cross-validation
  • LLM labels only for training and agreement checks (kappa 0.67)
Published limits
  • "Confirmed" tier: 3/6 on fresh test 2; rules fooled by "launches a dividend", "buyers flood in"
  • Weakest on new data: Cyber/Operational recall 0.53, Product Launch precision 0.55
  • Impact validated on firm-specific filings only
